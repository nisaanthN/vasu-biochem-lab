## What is BioPharm Lab?

BioPharm Lab is a **free, browser-based biochemistry study companion** aligned with the PCI B.Pharm syllabus paper **BP203T — Biochemistry** (2nd semester, 4 credits, 45 hours). It bundles six features into one workflow:

- 📚 **Interactive notes** — readable theory aligned with your syllabus
- 🧪 **Virtual experiments** — six hands-on simulations
- 🎓 **Adaptive quizzes** — 80 MCQs that target your weak areas
- 💬 **AI Tutor** — a curated chat assistant for biochem topics
- 📊 **Progress dashboard** — XP, streaks, badges, study recommendations
- 🏆 **Gamification** — 15 unlockable badges, 20 levels

You don't need an account. You don't need internet after the first load. Your progress is saved in your browser.

## Reading Notes

### Where to find them
- Click **Notes** in the navigation bar.
- The page shows all 5 units of the BP203T syllabus.
- Each unit lists its sub-topics. Click any topic to open the note.

### What you'll see in a note
Every note has the same structure:

1. **Header** — Unit number, topic title, estimated reading time, and XP reward.
2. **Learning objectives** — A box listing what you should be able to do after reading.
3. **Main content** — Written in clear, accessible language with:
   - **Bold terms** for important concepts
   - 💡 **Key points** highlighted in blue boxes
   - 🧠 **Mnemonics** in special cards
   - ⚗️ **Reaction schemes** in formatted code blocks
   - 🔬 **Tables** comparing related concepts
   - 💊 **Pharmacy connections** — clinical and drug-related callouts
4. **Inline checkpoint** — Many notes end with a quick MCQ to test understanding. Solving it correctly earns +5 XP.
5. **What next?** — A footer suggesting follow-up activities.

### How to earn XP from reading
- The platform tracks your reading progress automatically.
- A thin progress bar at the top of the page shows how far you've scrolled.
- Once you've read past 80% of the note and spent at least 60 seconds, the note is marked complete and you earn **+20 XP**.

### Tip
Don't just scroll fast — actually read. The 60-second dwell timer prevents fake completions, and you'll need to actually understand the material for the quizzes.

## Running Virtual Experiments

Six experiments cover the most testable biochem concepts. Each takes 7–15 minutes.

### The six experiments

| # | Experiment | What you'll do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Michaelis–Menten kinetics** | Slide Vmax and Km, watch hyperbola + Lineweaver-Burk update live |
| 2 | **Enzyme inhibition explorer** | Toggle competitive, non-competitive, uncompetitive — see how each shifts the LB plot |
| 3 | **Qualitative sugar tests** | Pick a sugar + a reagent, observe the colour change |
| 4 | **DNA transcription & translation** | Type a DNA template, step through codon-by-codon |
| 5 | **Glycolysis walkthrough** | Step through all 10 enzymes with a live ATP/NADH ledger |
| 6 | **Volumetric titration** | A full guided wet-lab simulation: weigh, dissolve, titrate, calculate molarity |

### How to use any experiment

1. Open **Experiments** from the navigation.
2. Click any of the six cards.
3. Read the **objective** at the top.
4. Interact with the controls (sliders, dropdowns, step buttons).
5. Watch the visualisation update in real time.
6. Read the **Observations** panel at the bottom — it auto-generates a summary based on what you did.
7. When done, click **Mark experiment complete** to earn +50 XP (+80 XP for the titration).

### Volumetric titration — special instructions

This is the most detailed experiment. Follow the procedure list on the right panel step by step:

1. **Weigh Na₂CO₃** — the balance shows a random mass between 0.13–0.17 g.
2. **Transfer to flask** — click Continue.
3. **Dissolve in water** — click Continue.
4. **Add methyl orange** — the flask turns yellow.
5. **Fill burette with HCl** — the burette fills with blue liquid.
6. **Record initial reading** — 0.00 mL.
7. **Perform titration** — this is the key step:
   - Click **Open stopcock** to start flow.
   - Watch the HCl Added counter rise and the flask colour change.
   - The flask is **yellow** when far from endpoint, **orange** when approaching, **red** at the endpoint.
   - Switch to **Drop-wise** when you're within ~2 mL of the expected endpoint.
   - **Close stopcock** when the colour just turns red — that's your endpoint.
   - Click **Endpoint reached → next**.
8. **Record final reading** — auto-captured.
9. **Calculate molarity** — the formula computes for you. Read the explanation in the Observations panel.

The calculated molarity should be very close to 0.1000 M (the "true" molarity built into the simulation). If you over-titrated, you can click **Run again with a new sample**.

## Taking Quizzes

### Two modes

**Practice mode** — Random 10 questions from a unit of your choice. Good for cramming.

**Adaptive mode** — Questions chosen by the spaced-repetition engine, targeting your weak areas and overdue topics. **This is the recommended daily practice.**

### How to take a quiz

1. Open **Quiz** from the navigation.
2. Pick your mode.
3. Read each question, click your chosen answer (A/B/C/D).
4. Click **Submit**.
5. See immediate feedback:
   - ✅ **Green** = correct
   - ❌ **Red** = wrong (the correct answer is highlighted in green)
   - The **explanation** appears below
   - A link to the related note is shown
6. Click **Next question** or **See results**.

### XP rewards

| Action | XP |
| :--- | :--- |
| Practice MCQ correct | +10 |
| Adaptive MCQ correct (weak area) | +15 |
| Perfect quiz (10/10) | +100 bonus |

### What happens after the quiz

- Wrong answers get scheduled for spaced repetition (SM-2 algorithm). You'll see them again on the right day to maximise retention.
- Right answers with slow response time get scheduled sooner than fast right answers.
- The dashboard updates with your new accuracy and weak areas.

### Tip
Don't rush. The platform measures response time and uses it to schedule reviews. Fast + correct = "I knew it" → longer interval. Slow + correct = "I worked it out" → shorter interval.

## Asking the AI Tutor

### How to use it

1. Open **Tutor** from the navigation.
2. Click a **suggested prompt** on the right, or type your own question.
3. The tutor replies in ~1 second with:
   - A formatted answer
   - **Citations** linking to relevant notes
   - **Quick-reply chips** for follow-up questions

### What it can do
The tutor is trained on ~40 curated biochem Q&As and a 120-term glossary. It can:
- Explain concepts (Michaelis-Menten, central dogma, ETC, etc.)
- Define terms (Km, zwitterion, holoenzyme, etc.)
- Compare two ideas ("difference between competitive and non-competitive inhibition")
- List steps (glycolysis, TCA, central dogma)
- Make clinical correlations (PKU, gout, diabetes)
- Help with calculations (ATP yield, pI calculation)

### What it can't do
It's a **rule-based tutor**, not ChatGPT. It doesn't:
- Make up new content
- Discuss topics outside the BP203T syllabus
- Have memory of previous conversations beyond the last 3 turns

If you ask something it doesn't know, it'll suggest 3 related topics from the curriculum instead of guessing.

### Each tutor question earns +2 XP (capped at 20/day to prevent farming).

## Tips for Maximum Learning

### 1. Use a consistent daily routine
- **Morning (10 min)**: open the dashboard, do your SM-2 due cards in adaptive quiz mode.
- **During the day (5 min)**: read one note from the recommended topic.
- **Evening (5 min)**: run one virtual experiment or chat with the tutor.

### 2. Don't skip the inline checkpoints in notes
They're small but they activate retrieval practice — proven to boost retention.

### 3. Use the tutor when you're stuck
Don't bang your head against a concept for 20 minutes. Ask the tutor, follow the citation, re-read the note.

### 4. Take the quiz immediately after reading a note
Schedule a 5-question practice quiz right after every note. The fresh retrieval cements the material.

### 5. Embrace the spaced repetition
Even when you "know" a topic, the SM-2 engine will bring it back. Don't skip it — that's how long-term memory works.

### 6. Aim for a 7-day streak first
The Iron Streak badge unlocks at 7 days. Once you have it, going for 30 days (Diamond Streak) is easier.

### 7. Compete with yourself, not others
There's no leaderboard. The Hall of Achievements timeline shows YOUR progress. That's all that matters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. All your progress is saved in your browser's LocalStorage.

**Q: What if I clear my browser data?**
A: Your XP, badges, and streaks will be lost. The notes/experiments/quizzes themselves don't change — just your personal stats.

**Q: Can I use it on my phone?**
A: Yes, but the experiments work best on tablet+ size screens (slider controls are cramped on small phones).

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Yes, after the first page load. Static content (notes, MCQs, tutor data) is cached. Refreshing requires connectivity.

**Q: Is the AI Tutor really AI?**
A: It uses three deterministic algorithms (intent classification, fuzzy search, glossary keyword scoring). No large language model. This is on purpose — it's 100% accurate on what it knows and never hallucinates. Read the **About** page for full methodology.

**Q: Why is my score showing 0% when I picked correct answers?**
A: This was a known bug in v1 that's been fixed. If you still see it, refresh the page or clear your browser cache for the site.

**Q: Can I download my progress?**
A: Not in v1. Future versions may add export. For now, your data lives in your browser.

**Q: How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?**
A: Feedback goes to the developer (Durga Bhavani, Assistant Professor). Check the About page for contact details.

## Troubleshooting

### "The site won't load"
- Check your internet connection on first load.
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge all supported).
- Disable browser extensions that might block JavaScript.

### "My XP isn't saving"
- Make sure your browser allows LocalStorage for the site.
- Check that you're not in Incognito/Private Browsing mode.
- Try a different browser to confirm.

### "The experiment isn't responding"
- Refresh the page.
- Check the browser console (F12 → Console tab) for errors.
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled.

### "The tutor gives me 'not sure' answers a lot"
- Try rephrasing your question with biochem terminology.
- Use the suggested prompts as templates.
- Remember: it's scoped to the BP203T syllabus only.

### "The reading tracker won't mark my note complete"
- Make sure you've scrolled past ~80% of the page.
- You need to have spent at least 60 seconds on the page.
- These guards prevent fake completions.
